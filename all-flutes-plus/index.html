Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our products and services.

How do I get to you?

We're a short walk from the nearest Underground stations. Full directions and a map are on our Contact page.

Do you buy second hand flutes?

Yes, we consider pre-owned flutes for purchase or part exchange. Send us the make, model and serial number and we'll be in touch.

Do you offer international shipping?

We ship worldwide. Fully insured delivery options are shown at checkout. Change your country from the selector at the top of the page.

Do you do repairs?

Yes. Our workshop handles everything from quick adjustments to complete overhauls. Contact us to book your flute in.

Do I need an appointment to try instruments?

Appointments are recommended so we can have a room and a selection of flutes ready for you, but walk-ins are welcome when a room is free.

Can I ship my flute to you, and if so how do you recommend I do so?

Yes. Pack the case inside a sturdy box with padding, and use a tracked, insured service. Get in touch first so we know it's coming.

What is your returns/exchange policy?

Please see our Returns Policy page for full details on returns and exchanges.

Can I hire a flute whilst mine is repaired?

Ask us when you book your repair and we'll let you know what's available.
