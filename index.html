The problem

Good businesses, let down by their digital front door

Most independent trades, salons, clinics and hospitality businesses aren't losing customers on quality. They're losing them before the first conversation even happens.

No website — or one stuck in 2015

No online presence, or a site that's slow, doesn't work on a phone, and hasn't been touched in years. Customers judge you on it before they ever call.

Missed bookings

Calls go unanswered mid-job, DMs pile up overnight, and there's no way to book online — so the enquiry goes to whoever answers the phone first. Usually not you.

No time to chase admin

Confirmations, reminders, and asking happy customers for a review all take time you don't have between jobs — so they mostly just don't happen.
